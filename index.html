Why radicals matter

Radicals are the alphabet of Chinese

Radicals are the building blocks that make up every Chinese character. Learn them first and you stop memorizing thousands of random shapes — instead you recognize patterns, guess meanings and remember characters far faster.

  • Origin & meaning — understand where each radical comes from.
  • Mnemonics — little stories that make them impossible to forget.
  • Lateral radicals — see how shape changes by position in a character.
