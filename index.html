方法 The method

How to learn each character so it sticks

HSK 1 is the first official Chinese exam (汉语水平考试). This is the exact routine the app is built around: keep a notebook beside you and let HSK 1 Lan power every step, for each radical and word you learn.

1

Read the origin, build a story

Open the character in the app and read its origin and curiosities slowly. Tie them to the shape itself, so it becomes a little story instead of a random drawing.

2

Trace it and say it out loud

Open the stroke-order view and write the character a couple of times, following each stroke. Say the sound out loud with the audio button as you write.

3

Write the pinyin, mind the tone

Next to it, note the pinyin and pay close attention to the tone. Getting the tone right from the very first time is what saves you later.

4

Add the meaning

Write the translation beside the pinyin, so the character, its sound and its meaning all sit together on the page.

5

Use it in a sentence

Underneath, add a Sentence: line and build one with the new word, reusing vocabulary you already know so nothing fades over time.

6

Test yourself in the app

Whenever you want to check what you really remember, come back and review in the app. That is where everything stays fresh.
